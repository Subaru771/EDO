How do I share? TV ↔ PC · PC ↔ PC · Phone ↔ PC
PC ↔ PC — No TV needed

Run Edo Desktop on Computer A and click Start sharing. Open its shown address on Computer B in a browser, enter the PIN, then upload from B or download files from A.

PC / phone ↔ Google TV

Run Edo on your TV, scan the QR code or open the TV address in a browser, enter the pairing PIN, and send or download files.

Phone ↔ PC

Start Edo Desktop on the computer. Open the computer's Edo address in Safari or another phone browser and pair. No TV or phone app needed.

The device running Edo Desktop or the TV APK is the host. The browser uploads files to the host or downloads files from it. Both normally need the same local network. HTTP is not encrypted.

SECURE CONNECTION HELP

Connect with HTTPS.

Run a quick network check without exposing your files.

How secure pairing works
1
Connect to the same network.Use the address or scan the QR code shown on your TV. Edo never requires an account.
2
Verify the TV certificate if HTTPS is on.Compare the certificate SHA-256 fingerprint shown on your TV with the certificate displayed or imported on this device. Do not trust a mismatched certificate.
3
Enter the six-digit TV PIN.Pairing happens after the browser successfully connects. You may also need to approve the device on the TV.

Local self-signed certificates may be rejected by browsers, especially on iOS or when the certificate lacks an IP-address SAN. Importing the public certificate does not automatically make it valid. Never disable certificate checks globally.

The TV controls approval requirements and can disconnect all devices. HTTP remains unencrypted. Do not port-forward Edo.

If this browser forgets its pairing, scan the TV QR code or enter the PIN again.
