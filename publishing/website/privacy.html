Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026.

Edo is a local file-sharing application for Android TV and other devices. Edo can transfer user-selected files to and from devices on the same local network. This privacy information is based on the available Edo 5.4.2 application source. It must be reviewed again before launch.

What Edo handles

  • Files and folders: Files you choose to send or share may be stored on the receiving device and made available to paired devices.
  • Pairing details: The receiving device manages local session cookies, trusted-device names and a hashed record of trusted session tokens.
  • Transfer information: The receiving device keeps a local recent-transfer history, such as filenames, sizes, actions and dates.
  • Network details: Edo uses local IP addresses to allow nearby device connections and network discovery.
  • Crash reports: The Android app can store crash details locally. Sharing those reports is initiated by the user.

No required account or Edo cloud

Edo does not require an account or a central Edo cloud service to perform LAN transfers. The current source package does not include advertising or automatic developer-operated analytics. App stores, device operating systems and websites used to download Edo may have separate data practices.

Security

Important: Edo's standard local HTTP transfer mode is not encrypted. Anyone able to intercept traffic on an untrusted network may be able to see transferred data or session information. Edo offers optional HTTPS, using locally generated certificates that may require manual trust and may not work in every browser. Use Edo on trusted local networks only; do not expose its ports to the internet.

Sharing and retention

Files you send are delivered directly to the receiving device. You control which devices you pair and what you share. Files and transfer history remain on the devices until deleted through Edo or the operating system, subject to the operating system's storage behavior. Trusted devices can be revoked through Edo's interface. Uninstalling Edo may not erase previously received files stored in shared Downloads folders.

Permissions

Edo requests network access for LAN sharing, notifications/foreground operation to keep transfers active, and storage or document-picker access for user-selected files. On supported devices it can ask Android to install an APK the user explicitly chooses. Edo cannot install packages silently.

Children

Edo is a general-purpose file utility and is not designed to collect information about children. Users are responsible for the files they share.
