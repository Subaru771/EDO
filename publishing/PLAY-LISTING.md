# Edo — Google Play listing copy (Android TV)

**App name:** Edo — Local File Transfer

**Short description (79 character limit):**
Send, receive and manage files on Google TV over your local Wi-Fi.

**Long description:**
Edo — Every Device. One Connection.

Turn your Google TV or Android TV into a simple local file-sharing hub. Send photos, videos, documents, APK files and folders from a Windows PC, Mac, iPhone or Android phone through its web browser. You don't need to install a companion app on the sending device.

How Edo works:
1. Open Edo on your TV.
2. Scan the QR code or enter the local address on your other device.
3. Pair using the PIN shown on your TV.
4. Select files to transfer over your local network.

Features:
• TV-friendly file browser to view and manage received files.
• Resumable uploads and progress reporting for interrupted transfers.
• Folder organization, search, previews and ZIP download from a browser.
• Trusted device management, pairing PIN, and optional TV approval.
• Optional HTTPS for setups that support trusting a local certificate.
• Works without a cloud account or third-party storage.

Important: Edo transfers files locally. Its standard HTTP mode is not encrypted, so use it only on a trusted home or office network. Optional HTTPS uses a device-generated certificate that may require extra trust configuration. Do not open Edo's ports to the public internet.

Designed for Android TV and Google TV; connecting phones and computers use a browser. Edo Desktop also supports standalone PC-to-PC transfers without a TV. The desktop companion is a separate download, not part of this Android TV APK.
